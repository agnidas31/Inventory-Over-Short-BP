1The problem

Raw audit totals pointed teams at the wrong stores

Stores are audited at different intervals, so a bigger dollar loss isn't always the bigger problem. Ranking by raw adjustment sent loss prevention to the wrong door.

Different audit intervals

A 90-day loss and a 30-day loss can't be compared as totals.

Two brands, two vocabularies

Thorntons and ampm used different audit categories.

Big stores lose more dollars

Ranking by dollars favoured busy stores, not badly run ones.

2The approach

Normalise by time, standardise by category, rank by exception

Make every store comparable first, then let the outliers surface on their own.

Ingest

Audit results, item hierarchy, store attributes and sales.

Standardise

Map both brands' audit categories into one hierarchy.

Align

Link each audit to the store's previous audit to get the period length.

Calculate

Over/short in $, loss per day, and loss as a share of sales.

Rank

Flag stores far above their region's normal, and worsening trends.

Report

Monthly, item, category and outlier-store views in Power BI.

The two formulas that change the ranking

Loss per day = audit adjustment ÷ days since previous audit
Outlier = loss per day > region mean + 2 standard deviations
